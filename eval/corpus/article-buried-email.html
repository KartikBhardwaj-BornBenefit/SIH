Grant notice

Applications for the 2024 cycle closed on 15 September. The committee reviews submissions in the order they were received and publishes results within eight weeks of the closing date.

Queries about an existing application should go to grants.desk@example.org along with the reference number printed on the acknowledgement slip.

Applicants who have moved since applying should update their records before the review begins, otherwise correspondence may not reach them.
