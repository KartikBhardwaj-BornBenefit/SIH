E. Obfuscated form, no helpful attributes

These inputs are pre-filled and carry meaningless names, so field-purpose matching learns nothing from them. Detection has to come from the value. Expect Aadhaar and email flags reported as “in value”.

F. Field purpose with no value

The opposite case: empty inputs whose labels make the purpose obvious. Expect flags reported as “asks”, with no value signal and no mask.
